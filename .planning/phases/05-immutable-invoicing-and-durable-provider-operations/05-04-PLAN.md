| Boundary | Required control |
|---|---|
| Browser/human/worker to financial authority | Caller-derived active tenant/capability, exact request validation, no implicit service-role authority |
| Database transaction to external provider or durable worker | Permanent business key, committed intent, fenced completion, explicit outcome_unknown |
| Private financial evidence to UI/log/release receipt | Closed summaries, recursive redaction, private retention, no secrets/hosted tokens |
| Local tests to operational enablement/release | Synthetic evidence labelled, immutable exact-head receipts and independent approval gates |
